# Play packet comparison: wiki vs minecraft-data (1.21.1)

Generated by `reader/wiki/compare.js` from `reader/wiki/wiki_packets.json` and minecraft-data `protocol.play`.

Only disagreements are listed. Both sides are first normalised for representation only: enums and bitmasks take their integer type, Optional/Prefixed Optional and option are marked optional, switch entries are dropped, a wiki length prefix before a single array is dropped, a same-typed X/Y/Z triple (or Pitch/Yaw/Head) becomes one vec3/rot3 entry, and 64-bit packed positions match position/bitfield. A packet is flagged when the normalised top-level field counts differ or the type sequences differ in order. Top-level minecraft-data fields are the container fields with anonymous containers inlined; array/option/switch count as one field each, and the contents of arrays, options and switches are kept as nested entries (not counted at top level). A flag is marked **likely benign** when the two sequences match once optional entries are removed (so only conditional fields disagree).

Compared 172 play packets. Flagged 28, of which 6 likely benign and 22 not benign.
